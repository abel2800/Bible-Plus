The practice

Three ways to meet the Word

Built for the same page, the same verse, morning after morning.

I

Read

Read bundled Scripture offline with book and chapter navigation, search, adjustable comfort settings, and parallel reading when available.

II

Listen

Listen to the public-domain WEB audio stream with chapter playback, queue, speed control, sleep timer, and caching where permitted.

III

Reflect

Keep notes, highlights, bookmarks, a prayer journal, and a reading streak in a private study space on your device.

Built for more voices

Audio Bible

Close your eyes.
Let the Word speak.

Start listening inside the Android app. WEB audio is streamed from eBible.org and chapters can cache as you play or download them.

01

Play a chapter

Use the reader controls or audio player to begin a chapter and keep the text close while you listen.

02

Keep the flow

Queue chapters, adjust playback speed, and set a sleep timer for a listening rhythm that fits your day.

03

Take it offline

Cache and download handling is available where the source permits it. The audio library itself is not bundled in the APK.
