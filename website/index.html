The practice

Three ways to meet the Word

Built for the same page, the same verse, morning after morning — with a gold reading experience designed for focus.

I · READ

Read offline

Bundled WEB, KJV, ASV, NASV, and Amharic packages. Book catalog, chapter grid, search, parallel reading, and adjustable comfort settings.

II · LISTEN

Listen deeply

Public-domain WEB audio with chapter playback, queue, speed control, sleep timer, and caching where the source permits.

III · REFLECT

Study & grow

Notes, highlights, bookmarks, prayer journal, reading streak, heatmap, daily goal, and Verse Studio artwork — all on your device.
